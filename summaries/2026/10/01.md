    *   **Khoriaty et al. (2026): "I've Seen How This Goes"** - Explores diversity measurement without embeddings, using a "surprise" metric from a fixed judge model.
    *   **Samuel et al. (2026): REDIPO** - Focuses on importing creativity from base models to re-diversify post-trained models, recovering significant SD through DPO pairs.
    *   **Li et al. (2026): "Narrative Flattening"** - Introduces three metrics to quantify "flatness" in LLM writing:
        *   **Thematic motion:** Measures embedding distance between sentences; LLMs show lower coefficient of variance (CV) compared to humans, indicating less dynamic storytelling.
        *   **Affective composition:** Classifies emotional content per sentence; LLMs show significantly fewer emotion-carrying sentences than human writing.
        *   **Stylistic variance:** Measures style distance between LLM and machine text; post-trained models become less stylistically diverse.
    *   **O'Mahony et al. (2024): "Attributing Mode Collapse in the Fine-Tuning of LLMs"** - Highlights that chat models are less diverse than their base models, with token-level collapses at SFT and SD being hardest at DPO.
    *   **Karouzos, Tan & Aletras (2026): "Where does output diversity collapse in post-training?"** - Investigates diversity collapse across different Olmo3-7B lineages (Think, Instruct, RL-Zero), showing varying degrees of SD loss at SFT and DPO stages, with RL-Zero retaining the most diversity but lower GSM8k performance.
    *   **Springer et al. (2026): "Annotations Mitigate Post-Training Mode Collapse"** - Suggests larger post-trained models are less diverse, correlation between fitting training data and reduced diversity, and failure of pretraining distribution to transfer. Proposes a method to preserve diversity during SFT by decoupling "plan" (R) and "response" (Q) generation.
    *   **Liu et al. (2026): "When RL Fails after SFT"** - Addresses RL issues after overtrained SFT, proposing interpolation of weights and resetting over-confident neurons.
*   **Directions and Next Steps:** The notes conclude with several potential project directions, emphasizing early collapse prevention, external data-driven recovery, objective function fixes, or building performance on highly diverse models like RL-zero.
